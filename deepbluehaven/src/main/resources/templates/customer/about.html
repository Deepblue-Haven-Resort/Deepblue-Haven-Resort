Our Heritage

Where Ocean Meets Exquisite Serenity

Founded with a passionate vision to showcase Vietnam's most scenic coastal frontiers, Deep Blue Haven began with a single oceanfront property in Nha Trang. Today, our collection spans across Nha Trang Bay, Phu Quoc Island, and Quy Nhon Cliff.

Every villa, suite, and garden terrace is positioned to maximize ocean breezes, golden hour sunsets, and natural light. We curate every detail—from hand-selected linens to private infinity pools—ensuring our guests experience ultimate peace and rejuvenation.

Our Philosophy

The Four Pillars of Deep Blue Haven

Our resort experience is shaped by four uncompromising principles of mindful luxury, dedicated service, and environmental stewardship.

Eco-Ocean Harmony

Sustainable, eco-conscious architecture designed to protect surrounding coral reefs, coastal flora, and natural marine biodiversity.

Bespoke Hospitality

24/7 dedicated butler service and customized itineraries crafted to make every second of your stay effortlessly memorable.

Culinary Artistry

Ocean-to-table seafood and world-class fusion dining celebrating the rich flavors of Vietnamese coastlines and global gastronomy.

Holistic Wellness

Deep-sea mineral treatments, warm volcanic stone therapies, and seaside sunrise yoga designed for profound mental and physical balance.
